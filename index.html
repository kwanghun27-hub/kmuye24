01 · ABOUT

What is K-MUYE 24?

K-MUYE 24 presents the twenty-four martial arts associated with Korea's late Joseon military tradition and the Muye Dobo Tongji.

This site brings together historical documents, martial practice, translation, research, and media. It is designed as a growing archive rather than a finished catalogue.

The first version focuses on a clear introduction. Detailed pages for individual martial arts, source texts, research notes, videos, and publications can be added progressively.
